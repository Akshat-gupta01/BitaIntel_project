For BITAINTEL, I recommend consolidating the entire vendor registration into 5 steps while retaining all essential company, product, compliance, commercial and verification information. Use conditional fields so vendors only see questions relevant to their business.
Step 1- login only
Account creation
Account: Authorized person's name, designation, email, mobile, password, OTP verification and acceptance of terms.
Step 2- Account & Company Profile
 company identity and legal details
Company details: Legal name, trade name, constitution, incorporation date, website, company description, logo, brochure and employee count.
Registered addresses: Registered office, corporate office, billing address, city, state/province, PIN/postal code and country. Add option add plant or office add
Business identity: GSTIN, PAN, CIN, LLPIN, Udyam, IEC, TAN, D-U-N-S and LEI, as applicable. 
Company structure: Branches, manufacturing facilities, authorized signatory and relevant directors/owners if photo available of marketing head/ purchase head / account head show multiple person profile add .
Basic documents: Incorporation certificate, address proof, applicable tax registrations and authorization documents.
Step 3 - Business Classification & Capabilities
What the vendor does and how the business operates
Business types: Manufacturer, OEM, contract manufacturer, trader, wholesaler, distributor, dealer, stockist, importer, exporter, service provider, consultant, agent or retailer.
Categories: Select from BITAINTEL's 20 main vendor categories.
Subcategories: Dynamically filtered based on the selected category; allow multiple selections.
Business activities: Manufacturing, trading, distribution, services, import, export, private label and contract manufacturing.
Manufacturing capabilities: Facility locations, owned/leased/contract facilities, processes, technology, machinery, installed and available capacity, R&D, laboratory, QC/QA and custom development.
Service capabilities: Service types, expertise, geographic coverage, equipment and delivery capacity.
Operating profile: Years in business, number of facilities, annual turnover range and employee count.
Facility information: Manufacturing licences, facility photographs and supporting technical documents.
Step 4 - Products, Services & Quality
Portfolio, specifications, certifications and technical documents
Products/services: Name, category, subcategory, brand, description, manufacturer, specifications, grade, standards, HS/HSN code and country of origin.
Supply information: MOQ, standard order quantity, monthly/annual capacity, available stock, lead time and sample availability.
Commercial product details: Indicative price, currency, price validity and target markets.
Product files: Images, catalogue, technical data sheet, COA and SDS, as applicable.
Certifications: ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 45001, WHO-GMP/GMP, FSSAI, CE, BIS and other industry-specific certificates, where applicable.
Regulatory approvals: Relevant manufacturing licences, DMF/USDMF, CEP and other regulatory filing details for pharmaceutical vendors.
Certificate records: Certificate number, issuing authority, issue/expiry dates, scope, applicable facility and uploaded evidence.
Quality systems: Testing capabilities, calibration, inspection, laboratory facilities, audits and quality assurance procedures.
Step 5 -Commercial, Trade & Payment Details
Buyer engagement, procurement, logistics and financial information
Business contacts: Sales, procurement, accounts and support contact persons, email addresses and phone numbers.
Commercial terms: Accepted currencies, payment methods, advance payment, credit terms, MOQ policy, sample policy, return/replacement policy and warranty terms.
Trade operations: Importer/exporter status, IEC, export/import products, sourcing countries, export markets, experience and annual trade turnover ranges.
International logistics: Preferred Incoterms, freight arrangements, customs clearance, export packaging, cold-chain capability and documentation support.
Customer and supplier references: Optional trade references, customer segments and relevant business experience.
Banking details: Account holder, bank, account type, account number, IFSC, SWIFT/BIC, branch, country and bank proof when required.
Authorized representatives: Directors, owners, signatories, business contact information, ownership information where necessary, and authorization letters.
Due diligence: Applicable compliance declarations, lawful sanctions screening, conflict-of-interest declarations and supporting evidence.
Step 6 — Documents, Review & Submission
Final checks, declarations and vendor verification
Document checklist: Registration certificates, applicable tax documents, address proof, IEC, licences, product catalogues, quality certificates, bank proof and signatory authorization.
Document metadata: Type, issue date, expiry date, facility, upload date, reviewer comments and verification status.
Review screen: Preview all five steps and edit any section before submission.
Declarations: Accuracy of information, authority to register the business, privacy consent, terms acceptance and authorization for lawful verification.
Submission controls: Save draft, previous, save and continue, preview and submit for verification.
Registration tracking: Vendor ID, reference number, submission date, completion percentage, pending documents and review status.
Status workflow: Draft, verification pending, submitted, under review, additional information required, verified, rejected, suspended or renewal required.
Post-submission: Admin remarks, correction/resubmission, downloadable registration summary and certificate-expiry reminders.
Vendor Registration
note
Conditional registration: Show industry-specific fields only when relevant.
Multiple selections: Support multiple categories, subcategories, products, facilities and certificates.
Progress saving: Allow vendors to save incomplete forms and resume later.
Smart validation: Validate email, mobile OTP, country-specific tax identifiers, file formats and required fields.
Security: Restrict sensitive banking and personal information, encrypt sensitive data where appropriate, and maintain access logs.
Verification: Keep account verification, legal entity verification, certificate validation and overall vendor approval as separate statuses.
This structure consolidates the full registration into five manageable steps without dropping the major information areas from the original specification.
